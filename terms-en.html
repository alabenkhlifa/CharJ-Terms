What Charj is

Charj is a free, open-source mobile application that displays information about electric-vehicle charging stations in Tunisia. Charger data is sourced from Open Charge Map under a CC-BY-SA license and augmented by community submissions.

Use at your own risk

Charger information may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect. Before driving to a charger: - Verify its existence and operational status by other means when possible - Check connector compatibility with your vehicle - Be aware of operator-specific pricing, payment methods, and access restrictions
